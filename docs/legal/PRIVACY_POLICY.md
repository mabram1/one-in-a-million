This policy explains what personal data *One in a Million* (the "Game", at
https://oneinamillion.skilliyo.com and as an Android app) collects, why, and your
rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)[[/ and the Swiss FADP]].

## 1. The short version
- You can play as a **Guest** with **no account** — that progress stays on your device.
- If you **sign in**, we store your email, an account id, your display name and your
  game progress so you can use the Game across devices.
- Your **"My Face" photo never leaves your device** — it is processed locally and stored
  only in your browser; we never upload or receive it.
- We do **not** sell your data and (currently) do **not** use third-party advertising or
  cross-site tracking.

## 4. "My Face" — on-device only
When you add your face to your character, the photo is selected from your device,
cropped/processed **in your browser**, and the resulting overlay is saved **locally**
(IndexedDB). It is **never uploaded to us or any third party**. Delete it any time in the
Game (Profile → My Face → Delete), or by clearing the app's site data.

## 5. Local storage / cookies
We use your browser's **local storage / IndexedDB** to keep guest progress, settings and
your face overlay. These are essential to the Game's functionality. We do **not** use
advertising cookies or third-party analytics [[confirm before publishing]].

## 6. Payments (subscriptions)
When subscriptions launch, payments are handled by **Stripe**. We do **not** receive or
store your full card details; Stripe processes them as an independent controller/processor
under its own terms and privacy policy. We store only your **subscription status** (active,
plan, renewal/cancel state) to unlock premium features. [[VAT/OSS handling via Stripe Tax.]]
