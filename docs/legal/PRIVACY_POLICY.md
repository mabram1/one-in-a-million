This policy explains what personal data *One in a Million* (the "Game", at
https://oneinamillion.skilliyo.com and as an Android app) collects, why, and your rights.
Because the Game is offered to users in the EU/EEA and Switzerland, it is written to align
with both the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Swiss Federal Act on Data
Protection (revised FADP).

## 1. The short version
- You can play as a **Guest** with **no account** — that progress stays on your device.
- If you **sign in**, we store your email, an account id, your display name and your game
  progress so you can use the Game across devices.
- Your **"My Face" photo never leaves your device** — it is processed locally and stored
  only in your browser; we never upload or receive it.
- We do **not** sell your data and (currently) do **not** use third-party advertising or
  cross-site tracking.

## 2. What we collect and why

| Data | When | Purpose | Legal basis |
|------|------|---------|-------------|
| Email address | On sign-in (Google or email link) | Create/secure your account, cross-device sync | Contract |

We do not collect data we do not need. Guest play stores progress in your browser's local
storage only.

## 4. "My Face" — on-device only
When you add your face to your character, the photo is selected from your device,
cropped/processed **in your browser**, and the resulting overlay is saved **locally**
(IndexedDB). It is **never uploaded** to us or any third party. Delete it any time
(Profile → My Face → Delete) or by clearing the app's site data.

## 5. Local storage / cookies
We use your browser's **local storage / IndexedDB** to keep guest progress, settings and
your face overlay. These are essential to the Game. We do **not** use advertising cookies
or third-party analytics.

## 6. Payments (subscriptions)
When subscriptions launch, payments are handled by **Stripe**. We do **not** receive or
store your full card details; Stripe processes them under its own terms and privacy policy.
We store only your **subscription status** (active, plan, renewal/cancel state) to unlock
premium features. Applicable EU VAT is handled at checkout.

## 7. How long we keep data
- Account + progress: while your account exists, then deleted/anonymised within 90 days of
  account deletion.
- Payment records: as required by tax/accounting law.
- Guest/local data: until you clear it on your device.

## 8. Your rights
You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your data, and to object
to processing. Contact **oneinamillion@skilliyo.com**. You can also complain to a supervisory
authority — in Switzerland the **FDPIC** (Federal Data Protection and Information
Commissioner); in the EU, your national data protection authority.

## 9. Children
The Game is **not directed at children under 16**. If you are under 16, please do not sign
in or subscribe without a parent/guardian. If we learn we hold a child's data without the
required consent, we will delete it.

## 10. Changes
We may update this policy; the "Effective date" shows the latest version and material
changes are announced in-app.
